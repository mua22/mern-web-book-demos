Plan Your Week Without the Chaos

Brightline is a small planning tool that keeps tasks, notes, and deadlines in one place, so your week stops living across five different apps.

Why People Switch to Brightline

Three reasons teams keep coming back.

One Shared Calendar

Every task and deadline lands on a single calendar, so nothing gets lost in a chat thread or a forgotten spreadsheet.

Built-In Reminders

Automatic reminders go out before a deadline hits, not after, so plans stay on track without someone chasing everyone down.

Works on Any Device

The same layout adapts from a phone to a desktop screen, so the plan looks right no matter what you open it on.

Built for Teams of Any Size

Whether it's a two-person project or a department of thirty, Brightline scales without asking anyone to change how they already work.
