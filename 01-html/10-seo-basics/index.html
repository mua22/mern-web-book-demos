Beginner Guide to Baking Bread at Home

Baking your first loaf of bread can feel intimidating, but the process comes down to a handful of simple steps: mixing, kneading, proofing, and baking.

Ingredients

Flour, water, yeast, and salt are all you need for a basic loaf.

Kneading the Dough

Kneading develops the gluten in the flour, which gives bread its structure.

How Long to Knead

Most doughs need about 8 to 10 minutes of kneading by hand.

Proofing

Proofing is letting the dough rest so the yeast can produce gas and the dough rises.
