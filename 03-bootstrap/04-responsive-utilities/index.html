Responsive Display and Flex Utilities

Resize your browser window narrower than about 768px wide (or open this page on a phone) to see the effects described below change live.

Display Utilities: Hiding and Showing Content

d-none hides an element completely. Adding a breakpoint, like d-md-block, means "hidden by default, but shown as a block starting at the md breakpoint." Combining the two lets you hide something on mobile and reveal it on larger screens.

You are viewing this on a narrow screen (below the md breakpoint). This box uses d-block d-md-none.
You are viewing this on a medium screen or wider. This box uses d-none d-md-block.
