State Variants

This page reacts to how you interact with it (hover, focus, click, disabled) and to your operating system's dark mode setting. Try switching your OS or browser to dark mode to see the card below change.

Button States

Hover over the button, click and hold it (active), click into it with Tab/keyboard (focus), and compare it against the disabled button next to it.

Dark Mode Card

This card uses dark:bg-* and dark:text-* classes. It has no extra configuration behind it — Tailwind's default dark mode strategy is media, so it simply follows your OS/browser color scheme preference.

Automatic Dark Mode

Light background and dark text right now, or the reverse, depending entirely on your system's color scheme setting — no toggle button on this page controls it.
